Cleveland police body camera violations, explained

Why don't officers turn on their body cameras?

It's the most common body camera violation in the division. According to a division spokesperson Sergeant Freddy Diaz, most activation failures aren't intentional. They come down to stress, split-second decisions, and a finicky button.

The job comes first and stress can override training

In emergencies, officers often act before they activate and only realize later that the camera was never on. High-stress calls trigger physical responses that push the camera out of an officer's mind, claims Diaz.

The button itself

Activation requires a firm double-tap. In chaotic scenes, officers think they've turned it on — but haven't. "They sometimes do try to turn the camera on and the button is not pressed deep enough and quick enough to turn it on. It's a two-tap process," explains Diaz.
