Cleveland police body camera violations, explained

Honest mistake vs. blatant violation

The division says it now weighs whether a failure was an honest mistake or a blatant violation. "Does this appear to be a blatant violation where the officer appears to just purposely turn off their camera or purposely not turning on their camera," explained Diaz, "or was it like, hey, the mitigating circumstances are a very high stress situation occurred very quickly, evolved really fast, and the officer just simply forgot." First-time accidental failures get a verbal warning, not a suspension. Blatant violations are disciplined more harshly.
